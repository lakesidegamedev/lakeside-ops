# Ops web changelog

## Unreleased

- **Kanban cards follow their support tickets.** A card can carry the ticket it came from (`ticket` and `ticket_role` on `kanban_add`, `kanban_update` and `work_report`; existing cards are linked once from "(ticket N)", "ticket N" or "Raised by Keir from ticket N", never from a bare "#N", a turn, a year or a version). Every linked card shows the ticket's live status, Open or Closed with the day it closed, on the work board, `kanban_get`, `kanban_list` and `kanban_brief`; a card still open after its ticket closed leads with a plain `ticket_warning`. Status reads are batched, cached for a minute, shown with their age when the ticket service drops, and read "ticket status unknown" instead of guessing.
- **Reconcile pass.** The ops web app runs it every 10 minutes (`KANBAN_TICKET_RECONCILE=off` stops it, `=dry` only logs). A ticket's own card closes when every ticket it names is closed, with the ticket's closing line in its history. A follow-up (set by `ticket_role: follow_up`, a "Follow-up from closed ticket N" title, a GitHub link, a capability, idea or project card, a card filed after the ticket closed, or a card with work reported after the ticket closed) stays open, relabelled "Follow-up from closed ticket N", and loses an assignee who has reported nothing for a day. A reopened ticket notes every card the pass closed for it and reopens the most recent one into the inbox. Every change is a card event with its reason; a second run changes nothing; at most 50 cards change per run; nothing ticket-related happens when tickets cannot be read. Dry run: `scripts/kanban-ticket-reconcile.js` (works on a snapshot copy; `--apply` to write), `GET /api/kanban/ticket-reconcile` (admin) or the `kanban_ticket_reconcile` tool (dry run by default).
- **Assigned means a live session.** A Now card whose assignee has reported nothing for 24 hours shows "no recent activity" (and counts as stale) on the board and in the tools, and the reconcile pass moves it to Next with the assignee cleared. Starting a card or moving it into Now counts as activity.
- **One word: Closed.** A finished ticket reads Closed (or "Closed, merged into N") and an unfinished one Open everywhere this app shows or returns a ticket's state, through one mapping (`ticket-status.js`). Changed: the ticket hub status chips and filter (Open / Closed), "Closed by Keir, 30d", the tickets list tabs (all / open / closed) and badges, the player receipt ("Your report is closed", "Closed <date>", timeline step "Closed"), the resolution notice ("Your support report is closed."), the ticket report page, the daily report, the Keir hub pills and receipt panel, the Keir supervisor's `ticket_evidence` and queue rows, and the throughput panel. Support tools add `ticket_status` (Open / Closed) to `support_get`, `support_list`, `support_search`, `support_with_images` and `support_resolve`, and `byTicketStatus` to `support_stats`. The stored `status` field is unchanged and still returned, because Keir's ticket logic compares it.
- **Support evidence lookup.** The ops-knowledge MCP gains read-only `support_evidence_lookup`: a support question maps to its verified definition, units, scope, AHDGame source reference and the one gamestate read to call, or a stated gap when that read is not in the caller's tools/list. Seeded for national GDP level vs growth, market listed vs filled vs funded demand, realized revenue vs operating profit, and action point grants vs spending vs balance. GDP level and drops route to `market_history` (never `country_fiscal`), all-sector or single-turn market questions to `commodity_market`, and reporter reads take no identifier because the Keir broker supplies the verified reporter. Read args follow the Rust gamestate for existing tools and game-contract v1 for `market_history`, `reporter_operating_results` and `reporter_action_points`.
- **Keir evidence pages.** The support service serves `GET /support/evidence/<id>` and `/support/evidence/<id>/chart.png` (mcp/evidence-pages.js) from the files Keir writes in `EVIDENCE_PAGES_DIR`. Ticket pages need a Lakeside account session (the web ticket form's check) and, on every request, all of: the live tickets row is still the one pinned in `meta.audience` (same `createdAt`, same reporter character in `reporter.profileUrl`), the viewer is that row's reporter (linked Discord id or AHD user), and the pinned character belongs to the viewer's own AHD account, so a reassigned or re-created ticket never shows the earlier reporter's data. Staff Discord ids (`EVIDENCE_STAFF_DISCORD_IDS`, default the owner and support delegates) are a separate path. Public aggregate pages (a public ping's one-off market report: no ticket, audience or tracker) need no sign-in; any other or malformed visibility is not found. HTML is rendered and escaped here, archived trackers ignore later files, responses are no-store and noindex with a CSP that allows only the page's own chart. `resolveChart(dir, id, { ticket })` gives the support update path a store-verified chart for its own ticket. Needs the Caddy route `/support/evidence/*` on lakesidegames.net to 127.0.0.1:19735.
- **Evidence on ticket replies: link only, stable retries, threaded replies.** `prepareEvidence` accepts a link-only presentation (manifest `chart: false`) and refuses it when `EVIDENCE_PUBLIC_BASE` is unset, so the answer goes as plain text. The link goes after the answer on the reply's first message; the private receipt is `linked` only with Discord's message id. `support_public_update` takes broker-only `update_key` (`keir-` plus 24 hex, a stable update key so a retried evidence answer resumes or reports the same update instead of posting again) and `reply_to`; the reply threads under that request only after a Discord read confirms it is a human message in the ticket's own channel, and with evidence only under the request the stage recorded. Replays return the stored evidence receipt. The sign-in note names the configured host.
- Reset Watch converts 2027 plant profits using their recorded currencies, uses funded trade receipts for sell-through, counts mothballed sites separately, and shows returns on operating and committed capital.
- **Sentry replaces GlitchTip.** New read-only Sentry MCP on 127.0.0.1:9740 (`sentry_issues`, `sentry_issue`, `sentry_event_lookup` for the 8 character ref on player error pages, `sentry_new_since`), unit `lakeside-sentry-mcp.service`. The dashboard error tile, daily report, issue triage and the new-issue Discord poller read Sentry (`SENTRY_AUTH_TOKEN`, org `lakeside-games`); the dead GlitchTip code and catalogue entry are gone.
- **Ticket replies read like chat.** A reply-mode `support_public_update` (and a resolution notice) longer than one chat line is now posted as up to four short Discord messages, about 1.2 s apart, instead of one wall of text. Same rules as Keir's own splitter: paragraphs, then sentence ends; lists keep their lead-in; code fences stay whole. The receipt keeps one update with the full text; the delivery record lists every part, and a retry resends only the parts that did not land. Card mode is unchanged.

## 4.6.1 (2026-11-05)

- **No developer pings for ticket PRs.** The #operations TL;DR for a PR that names a support ticket, and the "PR on the way" findings post, no longer tag the Developer role. Any PR mentioning a ticket triggered them, so routine agent PRs were pinging developers. Admin heal requests still ping, since they need someone to act.

## 4.6.0 (2026-11-03)

- **Economy page.** The AHD macro tracker returns as a dashboard page under Monitor instead of a standalone report. Three scores (economic health, gameplay, commodities) sit on one strip; six tabs split the rest: Overview (every red or amber measure, world macro and activity charts), Markets (commodity health and a supply/demand and real-price table for every good), Companies, Players, Countries and Fixes.
- Every measure is one table row: value, 12-turn change, healthy band and a trend line with the band shaded. A measure that counts in two scores is listed once.
- Time range defaults to the latest economy reset; "Whole world" shows every turn since the world began. Reset turns are marked on the charts.
- Server-rendered from one compact JSON feed (`ECONOMY_FEED_FILE`, default `/var/lib/ahd-economy/economy.json`) written by the tracker exporter each turn. No client script.

## 4.4.0 (2026-11-03)

- **Grouping.** Cards carry an `area` (a fixed list per product, e.g. economy, politics, turns, apps for A House Divided), a `class` (project, issue or idea) and an optional `parent_id` that puts a card inside a project. `kanban_add`, `kanban_update` and `kanban_list` accept all three; `kanban_get` lists a project's child cards.

## 4.3.0 (2026-11-03)

The work board's rules, from the kanban audit. These apply to agents (MCP tools) and people (the board) alike.

- **Inbox lane.** Next now means committed work with an owner. Cards filed into Next without an owner, and new cards by default, land in the inbox. Unowned Next cards nobody has picked up move to the inbox after a day.
- **Quiet cards are released.** A card in progress with no update for 3 days goes back to Next with its owner cleared and a `released` event. Untouched local backlog cards close as `expired` after 90 days.
- **Monitors.** Always-on agent cards are kind `monitor` (detected automatically from heartbeat volume). A heartbeat updates the card and records at most one history event an hour. Monitors stay out of lanes, briefs and staleness.
- **One name per agent.** Actors are normalized (claude-opus-5-5, Opus and Claude all become claude; `name:session` keeps parallel sessions apart). The shared token name is never used as an actor; a write without an actor is recorded as unnamed with a warning.
- **Cap on work in progress.** Three cards in progress per actor; `force: true` overrides. Keir is exempt.
- **Duplicate guard.** Adding a card that matches an open one in the same product returns the existing card (`duplicate: true`) instead of a copy; `force: true` adds anyway.
- **Plain progress, real evidence.** Progress that looks like a JSON dump is refused. `completed` needs `evidence_url` or `no_evidence_reason`. Titles that are questions for the owner are refused; owners are asked in chat.
- **Fixed kinds.** bug, feature, ops, balance, support, capability, audit, task, monitor; synonyms map automatically.
- **Shorter brief.** `kanban_brief` leads with the rules and returns your own cards (pass `actor`), released cards, blocked, in progress, next, shipped in 3 days and monitor heartbeats. `kanban_list` filters by `assigned_to` and `kind` and says when it truncated.
- **GitHub imports** carry the first 500 characters of the issue body and a kind from labels.
- The GitHub sync job runs board hygiene after each sync (`--no-maintain` skips it).

## 4.2.0 (2026-11-03)

The hub takes the Lakeside Platform look and every view was rebuilt around how the studio works now.

- **Look.** Geist type, near-black canvas, hairline borders and one teal accent, shared with the platform console. Older pages inside the hub pick up the same tokens.
- **Work board.** Always-on agent cards move to a status line. A strip shows what shipped this week, what is stuck, what is in progress, what is next and what is in the inbox. Cards that have gone quiet for five days get their own row. In progress is grouped by who holds it, then Next, Blocked and Done for the last seven days. Automatic filings and the backlog sit in collapsed lists. Cards never show raw JSON status; lane moves, release and notes live in the card dialog.
- **Library.** A category sidebar, a stat strip and cards that say what each report is and what it found, with a color per report type. Docs load only when opened, pages are compressed, and live trackers are no longer a section.
- **Tickets.** Live tab: Keir's 8-bit sprite and status, the models it uses, its watch sensors, and a scrollable row of open tickets with Keir's own summary, refreshed every 20 seconds. History tab: every ticket, searchable. Keir performance tab: 30-day results, runs per day, tickets opened and closed, model mix, and an hourly tracker kept in the data directory.
- **Descriptions are required.** `publish_report` now needs a `summary` (what the report is) and a `takeaway` (what it found) for a new report, and accepts `library_category` and `report_type`. A republish may keep the description already on file. The Keir shadow and bench publishers send both.

## 4.1.0 (2026-11-03)

Ops is now three views under one bar: the work board, the library and the ticket hub.

- **Hub bar.** A persistent top bar replaces the sidebar on every ops page, including the report viewer, tickets and the work board. It carries live counts per view, a search palette across reports, docs, tickets and board cards (`/` or `⌘K`), theme, and the signed-in user. Sign-in state is checked every minute and on focus, and a sign-out in one tab reaches every open tab. A tenant allowlist (`hub.consoleUsers`) controls who sees the fleet console link.
- **Library.** Published reports, older report pages and knowledge base docs in one catalogue with fixed categories, report types and labels, filtering, search, grid and list views, and a monthly publishing chart. A curated overlay (`library.json` beside the report manifest) gives each report a plain title without touching the publisher's manifest; uncurated reports are filed by keyword. Daily operations reports are excluded.
- **Report viewer.** Reports open inside the hub with a breadcrumb, type, newer and older within the category, copy link and full page. Report links inside a framed report move the whole hub. Shared report links still open as plain pages for people without ops access.
- **Knowledge base docs** render as pages with a table of contents and linked docs.
- **Ticket hub.** Open tickets with their latest player update, owner and linked PR; who has worked tickets in the last 14 days (staff and agents only); a compact feed of recent ticket work; pull requests linked to tickets with live GitHub state; weekly opened and closed counts and median fix time; and the full ticket history with search, filters and sorting.
- **Retired pages.** The other admin dashboard pages now redirect to the library. Public pages (status, player suggestion pages, ticket links, shared reports) are unchanged.
