# Conversation design evidence for Keir 11.1

Intercom reports customer-confirmed resolutions separately from cases where the customer leaves after an answer. This distinction matters when evaluating a change to Keir. Silence can tell us that a thread went quiet. A later reporter saying the answer helped provides evidence that a lesson worked in a different case. [Intercom's Fin reporting definitions](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/7837533-fin-ai-agent-reporting)

Slack keeps replies attached to the message that started a discussion. Participants can choose whether they receive more notifications from that thread. Keir applies those ideas to Discord tickets. The answer, chart and report link travel in one reply to the request. A request to stop updates persists across restarts. [Slack's guide to threads](https://slack.com/help/articles/115000769927-Use-threads-to-organize-discussions)

Lakeside's ticket history drove the choice of features. A question about whether to ignore a picker called for a sentence. A comparison across markets called for a finding and a table with units. A ticket tracker is useful when a future settled turn could change the answer. Its snapshots stay on the page, and monitoring ends after 24 hours or sooner when the ticket closes, a participant asks to stop or staff take over.
